Privacy Policy — How You Move

Last updated: 16 November 2026

How You Move ("the app") turns a recorded run into a poster. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Developer: Mikalai Malyhin
Contact: nikappservices@gmail.com

Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Your activity files, photos and posters stay on your device.
  • The app contacts the internet only for two optional features, described below, and for subscriptions if you buy one.

Data stored on your device

  • Activity files (.fit / .gpx) that you import: the route, elevation, time, heart rate and cadence they contain.
  • Photos that you choose as a poster background. The app copies the chosen photo into its own storage.
  • Posters and their settings, thumbnails, and your app settings.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting a run in the app deletes its files; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Data sent over the internet

The app sends data only when you use these features:

  1. 3D relief background. To draw the terrain around your route, the app downloads elevation and land-cover map tiles for that area from public datasets hosted on Amazon Web Services (Terrain Tiles; ESA WorldCover). The request contains the map tile coordinates of the area and, like any internet request, your IP address. It does not contain the track itself, your name or any identifier.
  2. Peak names. To find the names of summits on your route, the app sends the approximate coordinates of the detected high points (rounded to about 100 m) to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API. The track itself is not sent.

These services are operated by third parties and may log requests under their own policies: Amazon Web Services, OpenStreetMap and the operators of the public Overpass servers.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the two features above and for purchases.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older only): to save the finished poster to your gallery.

The app does not ask for access to your photo library. A background photo is chosen in the system photo picker, which hands the app only the photo you pick; on Android 13 and newer a poster is saved without any permission.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play, which processes the payment; the app never receives your payment details. To check whether a subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device and the purchase information from Google Play (product, purchase and expiry dates, country and currency of the store). It does not receive your name, email address, runs or photos. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Save, the poster image is passed to the app or gallery you choose. What happens next is governed by that app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes
